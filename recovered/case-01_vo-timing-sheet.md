## Statistics on screen — source

All three national figures come from one source: research by London Economics
commissioned by the **Department for Business and Trade** and the **Office of the
Small Business Commissioner**, published July 2025. The attribution is burned into
each stat frame.

- £26 billion sits in overdue invoices across UK businesses at any one time
  (average £17,000 per affected business)
- 14,000 UK businesses close each year because of late payment — 38 a day
- Affected businesses lose an average of 86 hours a year chasing payment

Source page: https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/late-payments-research-2/
Full research: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/late-payments-research-impact-on-the-uk-economy

Everything else on screen — names, companies, invoice numbers, dates, clause
wording, amounts — is fictional demo data and is labelled as such in-frame.

## Compliance notes

- No prices, plans, tiers or offers anywhere.
- Nothing implies payment recovery, chasing on the customer's behalf, or bank/email
  sync. The email thread in act 4 is where the *customer's* contract is buried — it
  is not a product integration, and the product UI shows no connected accounts.
- The product claim is specific rather than comparative: one register, an owner, a
  key date, a signed version, a delivery record and a next task with a name against
  it. No "better than" claims, which you can't substantiate and which invite scrutiny
  in a legal-adjacent category.
- "general legal information — not legal advice." holds on the final frame to 1:59.
